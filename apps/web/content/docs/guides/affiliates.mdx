---
title: Affiliates
description: Share OpenShip Cloud, track recurring commissions and request reviewed cash payouts.
---

Open **Affiliates** in your Cloud account to join the available program. You can
create referral links and codes, offer a customer discount, follow verified
payments and request a cash payout.

## Links and discounts

Choose a name, an eligible Cloud plan or all eligible plans, and an optional
expiration. The customer discount comes from your commission budget. For example,
a 20% budget with a 5% customer discount leaves you 15% of the eligible subtotal.
Your program's actual percentage and reward duration appear before creation.

Share the link or code. The customer reviews the offer, signs in, and chooses a
Cloud plan through the normal checkout. They can remove the referral before paying.
Revoking a link stops new referrals; accepted checkouts and existing referrals
retain their original terms. Create a new link to use different terms.

Only qualifying monthly and annual Cloud compute plans participate, starting at
$10 per month equivalent. Manage, prepaid PAYG and credit top-ups are excluded.
You cannot refer yourself or a workspace you belong to.

## Customers and earnings

The customer list shows anonymized customers, paid periods, next renewal dates,
expiry and payment history. Earnings are recorded only after payment verification
and adjusted for refunds. Renewals earn commissions until the original reward
period ends; changing plans does not restart that period.

## Cash payouts

The default minimum is **$50**. Your current minimum and available balance are
shown in the payout card; OpenShip may approve individual terms.

Enter an amount and select **Request payout**. Pending requests reserve funds and
can be canceled before review starts. OpenShip reviews each request and contacts
you through your account email to verify payment details. The Payouts tab shows
pending, under review, paid, rejected and canceled requests.

Available, pending and paid amounts are shown separately. Refunds can reduce the
available balance and prevent a pending payout from being approved. These are cash
requests, not automatic Cloud-credit purchases. Never share banking information
in a referral link or code.
